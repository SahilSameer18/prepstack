Why this order

Auth first. Anyone who forgets their password is locked out of the app for good. It’s small and independent of everything else.
Cleanups next. They take about a day and close open audit items.
Resume guide and progress record early. They’re a quick win and the foundation the rest builds on.
Notes before the planner. A planner pointing at thin notes is a weak product.

AUTH_PLAN.md Phase 4 (cookies), which waits for a domain.
